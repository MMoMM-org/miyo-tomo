Tomo Statusline — Pill Styles & Themes 🎨

Browser approximation of tomo/scripts/tomo-statusline.sh. In the terminal, rounded caps come from a Nerd Font (TOMO_STATUSLINE_CAPS=round, default); without one use TOMO_STATUSLINE_CAPS=square (block caps ▌▐) or none. Style via TOMO_STATUSLINE_STYLE=d|c|b. Colors are read live from the active /theme.
